# Privacy Policy for Symphonic

Symphonic is an open-source desktop client for self-hosted Navidrome and Subsonic-compatible music servers.

- **No Data Collection:** Symphonic does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, usage analytics, or telemetry.
- **Direct Connection Only:** Your server URL, username, and authentication credentials are stored locally on your device and are sent only to the Navidrome/Subsonic server you configure.
- **Contact:** For questions or support, please open an issue on our GitHub repository.
